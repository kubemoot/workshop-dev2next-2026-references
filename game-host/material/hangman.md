# Hangman

One player, the chooser, thinks of a word and writes one blank per letter. The other
player guesses one letter at a time. A correct guess fills in every place that letter
appears; a wrong guess adds a stroke to the gallows drawing. The guesser wins by
completing the word before six wrong guesses; the chooser wins otherwise.

## Rules a host keeps

- Show the word's blanks and filled letters, the letters guessed wrongly, and how many
  wrong guesses remain, after every guess.
- A letter already guessed does not count again.
- The chooser may not change the word during the game.

## A note for a crew playing chooser

The word must stay the same from the first guess to the last, and it must stay
hidden. A crew keeps nothing between turns except what is written in the
conversation, so a word it never writes down is a word it cannot keep.
