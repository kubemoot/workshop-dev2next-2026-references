# Rock paper scissors

Two players each choose rock, paper, or scissors at the same moment. Rock breaks
scissors, scissors cut paper, paper covers rock; the same choice is a tie. Play one
round, or the best of three or five.

## Rules a host keeps

- In a chat the two choices cannot arrive at the same moment: whoever reads the other's
  choice first can always win. A fair host chooses before it reads the player's
  message and never changes its choice after; a crew needs a rule that says so, and
  the players have to trust it.
- Keep the score after every round.
