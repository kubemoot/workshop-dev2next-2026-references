# Twenty questions

One player, the answerer, thinks of a thing. The other asks up to twenty questions that
can be answered yes or no, then guesses. A correct guess within twenty questions wins
for the asker.

## Rules a host keeps

- Answer only yes, no, or "sometimes" when the honest answer depends on the case.
- Count the questions out loud: "Question 7: yes."
- A guess counts as a question.
- The answerer may not change the thing during the game.

## Play well

Early questions should split the possibilities in half: "Is it alive?", "Is it bigger
than a bread box?", "Would I find it indoors?". Save specific guesses for the end.
